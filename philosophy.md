# Dzoglang Philosophy

Status: working principles, not a claim of scientific equivalence or a finished language.

## The Proposal

Dzoglang is a relation-first programming and artistic medium whose source is an
executable situation, accessible through images, sound, gesture, and exact
descriptions.

Our first runtime gives that situation a concrete substrate: an editable spatial
field whose laws, materials, boundaries, and excitations shape its evolution.
Programs are landscapes of possible interaction, not just instruction lists.

A program is a shareable situation. Its behavior can be encountered directly,
its commitments inspected exactly, and its relationships reshaped without
reconstructing everything from instructions.

We do not need to abolish symbols. We need to stop making symbolic description
the compulsory starting point.

## The Critique

Symbolic abstraction can amputate context when a label is treated as a complete
account of what it names. Instruction sequences can similarly become an
unnecessary narrative imposed on relationships better understood together.

The alternative is to encounter a situation through coexistence, interference,
and response before translating it into separate objects and operations.

This critiques compulsory description, not symbols or causality themselves.
Logic, constraint, dataflow, and parallel programming already exceed a single
linear narrative. Hardware execution order is not the same as a program's
conceptual organization. A field solver still executes instructions in time.

## Executable Images

An image here is not a bitmap, an icon, or a diagram standing for something else.
It is a situated whole: appearances, timing, context, and relations encountered
together.

Replacing function names with boxes and dataflow with arrows does not by itself
change the programming paradigm. The proposed shift is from assembling named
operations to authoring what remains related across change.

Instead of assigning two participants the same speed, establish that their pace
remains shared. Instead of specifying every frame, establish what yields, what
persists, and which transformations preserve an organization.

The running image is one manifestation of a program, not its complete meaning.
The program also specifies what could happen under other interventions.

Inhesion means that a participant's role depends on its situation. It does not
require every cell to affect every other cell immediately. Contemporaneous
relations can govern time-dependent behavior; neither simultaneity nor
contextual meaning establishes mathematical reversibility.

## Three Influences

### Hillman

James Hillman's distinction between symbol and image protects particularity.
An isolated symbol can become a dictionary entry; an image retains its context,
mood, manner, and internal relations.

Design consequence: preserve the situation while editing its relationships.
Do not assume that identifying every component explains the whole.

This is an inspiration for situated authoring, not a replacement for formal
semantics. Psychological meaning and computational behavior are not identical.
Hillman's image also need not be purely visual: words can preserve a situation
rather than reduce it to detached labels.

### Bohm-Hiley

Bohm and Hiley's active-information interpretation emphasizes form organizing
activity. Hiley's process-first work suggests distinguishing an underlying
organization from its multiple representations.

Design consequence: a small change in an organizing relation can redirect many
processes. Spatial, audible, temporal, and textual views can expose different
aspects of that organization.

This does not require quantum hardware, a wavefunction, or Clifford algebra.
Standard Bohmian mechanics includes particle configurations and their evolution;
it is not a computer architecture without objects or time. Quantum nonlocality
does not provide controllable instantaneous communication. In a second-order
formulation, the quantum potential has an associated force.

### Dzogchen

Dzogchen invites attention to recognition that does not immediately reduce
experience to conceptual labels. Nonconceptual awareness is not merely an
absence of thought or discrimination.

Design consequence: let people encounter and participate before demanding
classification. Make distinctions available when useful without treating them
as the only way to engage.

Primordial purity and spontaneous presence suggest, by analogy, an interface
that does not preassign every appearance to a fixed object category. Patterns
may arise through the interaction of authored conditions rather than a separate
script for each apparent entity.

An unpainted canvas is nevertheless a finite, allocated medium with chosen laws
and defaults. It is neither zero-bias nor infinite potential. Emergence depends
on those choices, resolution, initial conditions, and interventions.

Dzogchen is a contemplative tradition, not a computational theory. Dzoglang does
not claim to implement rigpa, produce realization, or translate specific
contemplative practices into interface commands.

These influences are distinct. Their synthesis is a design hypothesis, not an
established shared ontology.

## Communication

The proposed communication cycle is:

```text
Encounter -> Perturb -> Compare -> Commit -> Compose
```

1. Encounter an ongoing situation without first reading its schema.
2. Perturb it through movement, timing, interruption, imitation, or constraint.
3. Compare possible interpretations by experiencing their different responses.
4. Commit a relationship with explicit scope, strength, and tolerance.
5. Compose it with another situation and inspect what survives the change.

A demonstration is evidence, not a complete specification. Moving two forms
together could mean shared velocity, fixed distance, copied motion, or shared
arrival time. The system should expose ambiguity rather than silently decide.

A gesture is an utterance. The relationship it establishes is the program.

For the field instrument, painting changes a declared parameter or boundary;
exciting the field changes its current state. These are distinct utterances.
Comparisons initially use explicit branches of a saved situation, not automatic
inference of a gesture's meaning.

## The Field Medium

A field makes relation operational. Each cell evolves through the same local
laws, with neighboring values and authored boundary conditions. Large-scale
patterns result from their interaction, without a script attached to every
visible feature.

The first medium is a two-dimensional acoustic field. Pressure and velocity
evolve through material density, compressibility, and damping. Painted walls,
channels, and cavities shape reflection, propagation, and resonance.

Its scalar pressure is not a complex quantum phase, and its cells are not
Clifford multivectors. The numerical lattice approximates a continuum within
declared limits; it does not abolish discrete representations.

Local laws can produce globally organized patterns without centralized control
of each pattern. Influence still propagates over time. A boundary edit changes
the situation's future possibilities, not every distant value instantaneously.

Objects may later be recognized as persistent patterns of activity. The first
linear wave model does not produce autonomous agents, solid bodies, or stable
solitons. Emergence is a property to demonstrate, not an implementation shortcut.

Programs can organize interaction through interference rather than explicit
branches. This is one computational vocabulary, not a claim that every
conditional or exact symbolic task has a practical wave equivalent.

## Image As Source

Spatial fields can be authored as images: material coefficients, boundaries,
and initial excitations become inspectable layers rather than hidden object
properties. Their effects remain situated within the surrounding landscape.

The source is the field specification, not an ordinary color image alone. It
includes laws, channel meanings, scale, boundaries, inputs, and observation
mappings. A screenshot cannot recover all of these commitments.

A specification defines a situation; a checkpoint records one moment of it.
Saving a checkpoint requires every evolving variable and the solver's temporal
convention, not only the visible pressure. Storage preserves an experiment
without claiming that its apparent objects have permanent identities.

## Commitments

Three distinctions are essential:

| Distinction | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Appearance / commitment | What happens now / what the program promises. |
| Preference / requirement | What behavior tends toward / what must hold. |
| State / temporal behavior | Being aligned / recovering alignment after disruption. |

Hard requirements must hold within declared tolerances or produce an explicit
conflict. Soft preferences may remain unsatisfied; their residuals must remain
inspectable.

Recovery time is a behavioral claim that needs conditions and verification. A
coupling-strength control alone does not guarantee recovery by a deadline.

The field prototype initially validates model and numerical commitments:
positive material coefficients, explicit boundary behavior, finite values,
stable stepping, and declared input limits. A painted high-impedance region is
not an absolute wall; a fixed impermeable boundary is a different commitment.
General behavioral contracts remain future work.

Art preserves interpretive openness. Programming requires behavioral
commitments. The medium should support both without confusing them.

## Multiple Views

One relational structure can have several perceptual views:

- Space can reveal proximity, containment, or influence.
- Sound can reveal timing, coordination, or interference.
- A timeline can reveal persistence and transitions.
- Text can expose exact quantities, constraints, and composition.

In the field runtime, visible pressure and audible pressure probes share the
same evolving state. This is a shared origin, not a claim that light and sound
are the same physical field. The renderer maps values to colors; the audio path
samples, filters, transfers, and schedules values for a separate device.

Every view is selective. Its mapping must be explicit and trustworthy, not
presented as an exhaustive picture of computation.

Every semantic manipulation must have an explainable behavioral consequence.
Every committed consequence must have an inspectable representation.

Presentation changes must remain distinguishable from program changes.
Rearranging a workspace must not silently rewrite its relationships.

Painting material coefficients is intentionally semantic. Changing a palette,
viewport, or panel position is not. A source brush, material brush, and display
control must not be interchangeable without notice.

## Situated Composition

Context matters, but unrestricted global coupling makes programs difficult to
understand and unsafe to change. Boundaries remain necessary.

A reusable situated pattern carries relevant relationships, assumptions, and
exposed connections. Reuse requires deciding which relations remain internal,
which reconnect to the new environment, and which remain open.

For a field fragment, context includes edge conditions, material scale, spatial
resolution, and its coupling to the surrounding medium. Pasting a cavity into a
different material need not preserve its resonance. Such changes must be
inspectable rather than concealed behind a claim of image identity.

Abstraction is a deliberate selection of what survives relocation. It should be
supported by examples and counterexamples, not inferred from superficial
resemblance alone.

## Precision

The implementation may use identifiers, typed values, discrete events, ordinary
functions, and serialized files. Those are tools, not failures of the proposal.

Constraint solving, dynamical systems, and reactive programming already supply
many relevant mechanisms. The hypothesis concerns their organization into a
medium for situated authoring and communication, not their invention.

Waves are useful where they make relationships perceptible. They are not the
mandatory substance of every program.

Dense lattice stencils suit GPU parallelism, but their cost grows with cell count
and substeps, including empty regions. Bandwidth, dispatch overhead, boundaries,
and readback remain costs. Avoiding object collision queries in a wave model
does not demonstrate a faster or equivalent general-purpose game engine.

Finite grids have stability, dispersion, and resolution limits. They do not
guarantee absence of tunneling, exact conservation, or perfect boundary behavior.
Shared simulation state does not eliminate device latency or audio sampling
requirements. These limits must be measured and exposed.

Simultaneous relationships do not require instantaneous computation.
Bidirectional authoring does not imply reversible dynamics. Undo and replay
recover recorded history; they need not run a physical process backward.

Stability is not correctness. A stable result can violate the intended
commitments, and a correct program can oscillate or remain unsettled.

## Accountability

Authoring and debugging should share the same experimental vocabulary:

- Perturb a situation and inspect its response.
- Reveal the relationships responsible for a change.
- Compare a replay with one relationship disabled.
- Identify incompatible commitments without hiding them in a compromise.
- Branch an experiment without altering the original.

Interpretations must be confirmable. Automatic suggestions, including future AI
suggestions, must not become an opaque source of semantics.

Accessible inspection matters as much as visual immediacy. Color, motion, sound,
and pointer gestures cannot be the only ways to author or understand a program.

## Testable Hypothesis

Start with a small audiovisual instrument, not a new operating system.

The decisive question is whether people can communicate, predict, modify,
compose, and debug behavior through executable situations. Enjoying a display
is evidence of an instrument or artwork, not yet of a programming paradigm.

Compare against existing visual programming as well as textual descriptions.
Generalization beyond audiovisual composition remains an open question.

The first implementation is now a Rust/WGPU field instrument rather than the
earlier three-pulse browser sketch. Begin with wave propagation, painted
impedance and damping, fixed walls, and pressure probes. Verify the model before
adding moving matter or additional fields. Offline sound is an acceptable first
result; live GPU audio is a separate performance gate.

## Open Questions

- Which relationships are perceivable without extensive training?
- When does contextual richness become cognitive overload?
- How can a pattern preserve context without becoming impossible to reuse?
- How should uncertainty and conflicting interpretations be experienced?
- Which tasks benefit from this medium, and which remain clearer in text?
- Can the interaction scale beyond small systems without hiding dependencies?
- Can shared-field observation remain understandable across visual and audio rates?
- Which boundary conditions and numerical limits must travel with a field fragment?
- When do dense fields help more than sparse objects or explicit constraints?

Uncertainty: medium that this produces a distinctive programming medium; high
that it generalizes to everyday computing.
